Durga Puja in West Bengal is one of the most celebrated folk festivals, drawing millions of devotees and tourists every year with its grand pandals, cultural performances, and rituals.

Rajasthan is famous for Ghoomar, Kerala for Theyyam and Mohiniyattam, Punjab for Bhangra, and Gujarat for Garba — each representing their state's unique cultural spirit.

India has 8 officially recognized classical dance forms: Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kuchipudi, Odissi, Manipuri, Mohiniyattam, Kathakali, and Sattriya.
