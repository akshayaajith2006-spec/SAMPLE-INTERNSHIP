Discover the Folk Arts & Festivals of India

India is home to a breathtaking variety of folk arts and festivals that reflect the rich cultural heritage of its diverse communities. From vibrant dance forms to colorful celebrations, every state has its own unique traditions passed down through generations.

Exploring these traditions helps us appreciate India's unity in diversity and the deep-rooted cultural identity of its people.
